# daze_prone（发呆趴趴）生成提示词

Use case: identity-preserve
Asset type: transparent desktop-pet animation strip for Qt/QML
Primary request: Create exactly one coherent horizontal strip of SIX distinct animation frames of the exact same giant panda from the supplied canonical and prone-pose references, performing a quiet `daze_prone` loop: lying fully prone with the broad fluffy head resting very low between or on the two black forepaws, stocky body extending behind, staring blankly and peacefully ahead as if zoning out.
Frame sequence, left to right: 1) neutral blank forward stare, 2) tiny natural inhale with shoulders/body rising imperceptibly, 3) eyelids very slightly heavier with a tiny unfocused gaze drift, 4) one slow soft blink nearly closed, 5) eyes reopen into the same gentle blank stare with a tiny ear/cheek settle, 6) return almost exactly to frame 1 for a seamless loop.
Style/medium: highly faithful photorealistic panda cutout matching the canonical base and real reference photos; natural dense fur and realistic anatomy; absolutely not cartoon, chibi, plush, sticker, or illustration.
Composition/framing: six complete separated full-body prone poses in one row, equal scale, identical camera angle, stable body anchor and baseline, consistent bounding box, generous transparent gutter between frames, no overlap and no cropping.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background with clean alpha.
Identity invariants: preserve the same round broad head, compact black ears, exact black eye-patch shapes and placement, small dark eyes, white muzzle, broad black nose, subtle mouth, shoulder band, stocky torso, short thick black forelimbs, black-and-white distribution, fur density, coloration, and realistic proportions. Preserve identity and pose construction across all six frames; only the listed micro-motion may change.
Constraints: no wooden platform, logs, trees, habitat, floor, contact shadow, drop shadow, glow, motion lines, symbols, text, labels, frame numbers, borders, watermark, UI, extra props, extra limbs, detached fur or effects. No smiling, open mouth, tongue, waving, sleeping, or walking. Output only the six-pose strip on true transparency.

参考图角色：`references/canonical-base.png` 为身份与渲染锚点；`references/papa/papa-front-prone.jpg` 为主姿态；`papa-three-quarter-prone.jpg` 与 `papa-side-prone.jpg` 只补充体积和肢体结构；`references/layout-guides/waiting.png` 只作为六槽布局，禁止复制可见像素。
